About BlitzLearn

BlitzLearn is an AI-powered learning platform that turns your own notes into an interactive study assistant, tests what you know, and helps you close the gaps in your skills with hands-on practice and a personalized learning path.

15 languages supported in chat 2 quiz formats — MCQ and quiz-style 4 lab types — Code, Cloud, Finance, General

Chat with your own notes

Upload a PDF, pick from pre-loaded books, or point BlitzLearn at a YouTube lecture, and ask it questions the way you would a tutor who has actually read the material.

  • Bloom's-level tuning — get answers pitched at the level of understanding you're working on, from basic recall to deeper analysis.
  • Study modes — switch between a formal, exam-focused explanation style or a casual, regional Hinglish "Vibe" mode.
  • 15 languages — chat in the Indian language you're most comfortable with.
  • Topic prioritization — see which topics in your material are most likely to matter for exams.

Test yourself

Generate practice tests and quizzes directly from your notes, in whichever format suits how you want to revise:

  • MCQ tests — classic four-option multiple choice questions.
  • Quizzes — true/false and fill-in-the-blank questions for quick recall practice.
  • Difficulty and length control — choose how hard the questions are and how many you want.
  • Full answer review — go back through any past test or quiz, question by question.

Know where you actually stand

Right after you sign up, BlitzLearn runs a skill assessment based on the skills you declared, and keeps a running competency score for each one after that — updated by every test, quiz, and lab you complete.

  • Skill radar chart — see your current standing across every skill at a glance.
  • Gap analysis — compare where you are against where you're expected to be.
  • Progress over time — track how your overall competency has moved across every assessment you've taken.
  • Test history — revisit every past attempt and answer whenever you want.

Practice with hands-on labs

When a skill needs work, BlitzLearn queues up a lab for it — your lowest-scoring, highest-priority skills come first. Each lab is shaped to match the skill:

  • Code labs — write and run a working solution in an in-browser editor.
  • Cloud labs — describe the steps or configuration for an infrastructure task.
  • Finance labs — work through a real calculation to a final answer.
  • General labs — write up an applied, practical response.

Every lab opens with a short theory section and worked examples before the task itself, and your submission is graded with a score, feedback on mistakes, and suggestions for what to improve.

Follow a roadmap built around you

Your skill gaps are matched against real course recommendations, so you always know what to study next. If nothing matches a particular gap, BlitzLearn suggests practical sub-topics instead — your roadmap is always prioritized by whichever gap needs closing most.

Stay consistent

Your profile keeps a day-by-day activity heatmap of your tests, quizzes, and labs, along with your current streak, longest streak, total active days, and learning hours — so you can see your consistency building up over time, not just your scores.
