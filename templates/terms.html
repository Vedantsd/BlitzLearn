BlitzLearn is an AI-enabled skill intelligence and learning platform. It combines a retrieval-augmented chat assistant built on your own notes with a persistent, AI-scored competency model that tracks what you actually know, identifies gaps against role-based targets, assigns hands-on labs and recommended courses to close those gaps, and gives department trainers and administrators a forecasted view of where skills across the organization are headed.

2Account Types & Roles

BlitzLearn has three kinds of accounts, each with a different scope of access:

  • Learner accounts are created via Firebase Authentication during signup and are verified by a one-time email code before use.
  • Trainer accounts are provisioned by an administrator, not self-registered, and are scoped to a single department. A trainer can only view and act on learners within their own department.
  • Admin accounts are platform-wide and are used for user, trainer, and department management, reporting, and oversight.

Trainer and Admin accounts use a separate, session-based authentication system from learner accounts and are not created through the public signup flow.

5Skill Assessments, Labs & Competency Scoring

A core part of BlitzLearn is a running, per-skill competency score that is built and updated as you use the Service. Please be aware of how this works:

  • An AI-generated skill assessment runs shortly after signup, based on the skills you declared, and produces your initial competency scores
  • Every subsequent test, quiz, or hands-on lab you complete and pass can further update these scores
  • Your competency scores are compared against a designation-aware target to calculate a skill gap, which drives your personalized roadmap and the labs queued for you
  • Roadmap recommendations may link to external course catalogues, such as iGOT Karmayogi or NSSTA TPAC training resources, or may be AI-generated fallback topics when no matching external course is available
  • Because these scores are used for training recommendations and, at a department level, for organizational reporting, you agree to complete assessments and labs yourself and not to falsify or artificially inflate your responses
