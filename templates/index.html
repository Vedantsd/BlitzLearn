The problem

iGOT has the courses. Officials still can't find the right one.

India's statistical system is moving fast toward AI, ML, GIS, cloud, and modern statistical methods — and officials in data collection, processing, analysis, and policy support need to keep upskilling to keep pace.

The iGOT Karmayogi platform already holds a vast course repository, but there's no mechanism that looks at an individual's actual job role, current competencies, and future requirements and says: "start here." Manual appraisal-based gap-finding is infrequent, self-assessment is subjective, and trainers still build assessments by hand.

BlitzLearn replaces that guesswork with a continuous, AI-driven competency engine — purpose-built for Official Statistics.

The solution

One platform, six jobs done for the official

BlitzLearn builds a live competency profile per official, maps it against Statistical, Technical, Digital Governance, and Behavioural competency frameworks, and keeps recommendations moving as skills grow.

Competency Profiling

Auto-builds a profile from designation, department, job role, qualifications, experience, and prior trainings.

AI Skill-Gap Analysis

Benchmarks the official against Statistical, Technical, Digital Governance & Behavioural competency frameworks.

iGOT & NSSTA Integration

Pulls the live course catalogue via API and matches NSSTA TPAC training programmes to identified gaps.

MCQs from your own notes

Upload any PDF — lecture notes, a manual, a course reader — and the RAG + Gemini pipeline writes a graded test from it.

Personalized Roadmaps

Ranks courses and TPAC programmes by predicted relevance to the specific, individual gap — not designation alone.

Dual Dashboards

A learner view for progress & recommendations; an admin view for workforce-wide competency and training-effectiveness analytics.

How it works

From login to continuous skill growth

The same seven states every learner moves through — this is the actual state machine behind BlitzLearn.

01

Login

Secure Firebase authentication gives access to the personalized platform.

02

Dashboard

One overview of learning status, assessments, and recommendations.

03

Skill Assessment

An AI-generated test evaluates existing knowledge and produces competency scores.

04

Gap Identification

Current competencies are compared against role-based targets to surface specific gaps.

05

Personalized Content

iGOT Karmayogi courses and NSSTA TPAC programmes are recommended against the gap.

06

Learning Experience

The official engages with AI chat, quizzes, MCQs, and tests tuned to their Bloom's level.

07

Continuous Skill Growth

Every test taken re-scores competency and refreshes the roadmap — the loop never really ends.
